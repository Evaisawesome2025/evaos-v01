REAL Short read of lessons_learned.md and two coach notes. 2026-09-29.

  • A working demo is not a sale. Software you can click is a capability. Strangers paying is the evidence.
  • Kill a weak offer before you spend outreach. The contractor quote-follow-up idea died on a desk sample. That was the point of the kill rule.
  • People paying for a category is not people paying us. Backer updates, escape-room packs, and bid PDFs all died that way.
  • A payment link that “works” can be the wrong seller. Check seller, price, and offer before anything points at checkout. That miss already happened once.
  • Mail that lands in spam is not a demand test. ListingLift’s cold wave failed delivery. Do not call that “nobody wants it.”
  • A removed post is not a finished test either. The Reddit post came down. It does not count as a clean public exposure.
